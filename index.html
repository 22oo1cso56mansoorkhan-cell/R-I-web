About Robotics & Innovation Club

Fostering hands-on engineering, creative problem solving, and competitive spirit at CBIT.

The Robotics & Innovation (R&I) Club of CBIT is one of the most active technical clubs on campus. It provides students with a dynamic platform to explore robotics through creativity, teamwork, hands-on learning, and practical engineering.

R&I has developed a strong presence in combat and competitive robotics, having designed high-performance bots and participated in numerous prestigious intercollegiate technical competitions across the nation.

COMPETITIONS

ROBOVANZA Competitions

Explore the 6 core robotics challenges featured at ROBOVANZA.

Combat Arena

Robo Wars

High-intensity combat bot battle inside a reinforced polycarbonate arena. Custom spinners, flippers, and wedges fight for domination.

Sports & Agility

Robo Soccer

Custom mobile robots compete in a fast-paced football match, testing driver precision, ball handling mechanisms, and team tactics.

Pusher Challenge

Robo Sumo

Heavy-traction autonomous or manual bots attempt to push their opponent out of a elevated circular ring (Dohyo).

Terrain Traversal

Robo Rover

All-terrain vehicle challenge navigating steep inclines, gravel beds, water traps, and rocky obstacle tracks.

Autonomous Racing

Line Follower

Autonomous bots race along complex black vector tracks with sharp curves, grid intersections, and speed zones.

Aerial Racing

FPV Racing

High-speed pilot drone obstacle course featuring illuminated 3D gates, tight chicanes, and night racing conditions.
